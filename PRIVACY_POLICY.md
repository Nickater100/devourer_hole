# Privacy Policy for Angry Ball

Last updated: November 11, 2026

**Angry Ball** ("the App") is provided by Nickater. This page explains what data the App collects, why, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it.

## 1. Data We Collect

### If you sign in with Google (optional)
Signing in is only needed to appear on the global leaderboard. When you sign in with Google, the App sends a Google sign-in token to our own server, which verifies it and stores:
*   **Your Google account identifier**, used to recognize your leaderboard entry.
*   **Your display name and profile photo URL**, shown next to your score on the public leaderboard.
*   **Your best survival score and its stats** (cubes eaten, best combo, game length), shown on the leaderboard and used to reject impossible scores.

We do **not** store your email address. This data is used only for the leaderboard and account management. It is not sold, not shared with third parties and not used for advertising. It is stored on a server operated by us (Oracle Cloud infrastructure).

### Advertising (all users)
The App shows ads through **Unity LevelPlay** (formerly ironSource), a mediation platform that selects each ad from these ad networks: **ironSource Ads**, **Unity Ads**, **Liftoff (Vungle)** and **Google AdMob**. To serve and measure ads and prevent fraud, these services may collect:
*   **Device identifiers**, such as the Android advertising ID.
*   **Approximate location**, inferred from your IP address.
*   **App interactions**, such as ads viewed and clicked.
*   **Diagnostics**, such as crash logs and performance data.

This data is shared with Unity (LevelPlay, ironSource Ads and Unity Ads), Liftoff and Google. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads / Privacy → Ads).
How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites
Unity / ironSource privacy policy: https://unity.com/legal/game-player-and-app-user-privacy-policy
Liftoff privacy policy: https://liftoff.io/privacy-policy/

The App does not request location permissions and does not access your contacts, files, camera, or microphone.

## 2. Data Stored on Your Device
Game progress (coins, campaign progress, unlocked skins, and upgrades) is stored only on your device. It is not uploaded. Only if you sign in, your best survival score is sent to the leaderboard server.

## 3. Security
All data sent by the App is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).

## 4. Children's Privacy
The App is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

## 5. Account and Data Deletion

**Deleting it in the App:** open Settings (⚙) on the start screen and tap **Delete account**. This immediately and permanently removes your account from our server: your Google account identifier, display name, photo URL, best score and its stats, and all your sessions. Nothing is kept.

**Deleting it from the web or by email:** see https://astragal.duckdns.org/angryball/borrar-cuenta, or send an email to *nicosuasnavar@gmail.com* with the subject "Angry Ball - Data Deletion Request" and the name that appears on the global leaderboard. We will process your request within 7 business days.

Local game data (coins, unlocked skins, campaign progress) is removed when you uninstall the App or clear its data in Android Settings.
